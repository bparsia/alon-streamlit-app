\begin{example}
Let John be agent 1, Alice be agent 2, and Matt be agent 3. The three agents have the following actions available to them in the moment immediately prior to Sam's death:\\
\vspace{-\parskip}\nointerlineskip
\begin{minipage}{.45\linewidth}
    \begin{align*}
        pt_1 &\quad \text{John puts poison in Sam's tea}\\
        ss_2 &\quad \text{Alice shoots Sam}\\
        wl_3 &\quad \text{Matt writes a letter in his bedroom}
    \end{align*}
\end{minipage}
\begin{minipage}{.4\linewidth}
    \begin{align*}
        mt_1 &\quad \text{John puts milk in Sam's tea}\\
        is_2 &\quad \text{Alice ignores Sam}\\
        lb_3 &\quad \text{Matt leaves his bedroom}
    \end{align*}
\end{minipage}

Let $\varphi$ be the proposition that Sam dies. In the actual scenario, John poisons Sam's tea, Alice shoots Sam, and Matt writes a letter in his room, i.e. $do(pt_1ss_2wl_3)$. The shaded area below represents this actual scenario, which results in the history $h_1$.
